# Product: Stitchwork

Stitchwork is a Frankenstein answer layer for small regulated teams. It sews four kinds of disconnected company knowledge (wiki pages, tickets, chat exports and spreadsheets) into one body and lets a person ask questions of it.

Every answer obeys four promises. These are the product, not decoration:

1. Permissions before answers. A passage the asker may not open is never ranked, quoted or counted in anything the asker sees.
2. Restricted looks exactly like nonexistent. For a given person, the response to a question is the same whether a restricted document exists or not.
3. Every sentence is stitched to a source. Each answer sentence carries a citation to the exact source, location and version, and the time its access was re-checked.
4. Every step goes in the Crypt. A hash-chained, sealed audit log records the request, the access decision and the release. If the log cannot be written, no answer is released.

The Walnut stitches hold the creature together. When the optional Walnut service runs, every sentence must also pass Walnut's grounding wall against the passages it cites, every citation carries a Walnut source pointer, and every Crypt event is sealed in Walnut's durable ledger first. If the stitches fail, nothing is released. Walnut is a pre-existing engine by the same team, vendored unchanged and disclosed as such.

A further feature makes the theme earn its keep: ghost versions. Superseded document versions (ghosts) are detected and excluded from answers, and the asker is told which ghost was skipped in favour of the living version.

## Users

- Knowledge workers (claims analysts, account managers, people partners, contractors) who ask questions.
- Compliance officers and data stewards who read the Crypt, verify it, and exorcise ghost documents.
- Developers who call the serverless API.

## Theme rules

- Halloween styling is tasteful and functional: stitches are citations, ghosts are stale versions, the Crypt is the audit log. No gore, no jump scares, no flashing.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Text contrast meets WCAG AA.

## Data

All bundled data is synthetic and fictional. The company "Hollowmere Assurance" and its people do not exist. Never add real personal data or real company names.
